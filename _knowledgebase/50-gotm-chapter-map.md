# Gardens of the Moon — Beat Map

`#spoiler:gotm`. **This file is the game's spine.** Every game chapter maps to a range of beats
here. Beats are compressed plot facts in our own words — expand them into scenes, never copy prose.

Structure of the novel: a Prologue, seven internal "Books", 24 chapters, an Epilogue.
Roughly 209,000 words.

## Prologue — Malaz City, 1154 Burn's Sleep
- Young **Ganoes Paran** watches the Mouse Quarter burn while the Empire purges its wax-witches.
- A scarred Bridgeburner gives him the warning that frames the series: the best life is the one the
  gods never notice. Paran wants to be a soldier anyway.
- **Surly** takes the Claw and the throne; from here she is **Empress Laseen**.

## Book One — Pale (Ch. 1–4)
1. Two years later, Itko Kan. **Cotillion** and **Shadowthrone** possess a fisher girl; the Hounds
   butcher a cavalry troop as cover. **Adjunct Lorn** and Lieutenant Paran investigate; Lorn admits
   the massacre was a screen. The girl, now calling herself **Sorry**, enlists for Genabackis.
2. Genabackis, after the **Siege of Pale**. The assault on **Moon's Spawn** failed; **Tayschrenn**'s
   sorcery killed Malazan mages including Calot and Nightchill. **Whiskeyjack**'s Bridgeburners are
   down to about forty. **Quick Ben** binds the dying mage **Hairlock**'s soul into a puppet.
   **Tattersail** lays a Deck reading: Oponn and Anomander Rake are in play.
3. Paran arrives as captain over Whiskeyjack's squad, secretly ordered to watch Sorry. Sorry knifes
   him in an alley. **Oponn's Coin** keeps him from dying.
4. Paran stands at **Hood's Gate** and bargains his way back. A Hound attacks Tattersail; Paran's
   luck-blessed sword *Chance* wounds it — which should be impossible. **Dujek** orders the
   Bridgeburners to infiltrate **Darujhistan**.

## Book Two — Darujhistan (Ch. 5–7)
5. **Kruppe** dreams himself into six aspects. **Crokus** burgles the D'Arle estate and falls for
   **Challice**. A spinning coin turns a crossbow bolt aside.
6. **Crone** carries Rake's offer to **Baruk**; Rake proposes alliance against the Empire.
   **Rallick Nom** twists a poisoned bolt away from Lady Simtal and kills Councilman Lim instead.
7. **K'rul** speaks to Kruppe in dream and gives him elder fire. Crokus shows Kruppe the two-headed
   coin; it disrupts sorcery. Baruk orders the Coinbearer protected — or removed.

## Book Three — The Mission (Ch. 8–10)
8. A **Black Moranth** brings Whiskeyjack intelligence and an extraction window. The squad begins
   mining Darujhistan's gas lines with **Moranth munitions**. Hairlock slips his leash.
9. **Toc the Younger** finds the dead on the Rhivi Plain. **Onos T'oolan (Tool)** rises to escort
   Lorn. Tattersail and Paran make their own alliance; Lorn confronts Tattersail about the Mouse
   Quarter.
10. Tattersail flees toward Darujhistan, hits a warren dead zone Tool has made, and is caught by
    the grief-mad giant **Bellurdan**. Both burn in the detonation. A child walks out of the ash.

## Book Four — Assassins (Ch. 11–13)
11. Kruppe dreams the deep past; a Rhivi woman gives birth and a white-fox tattoo passes to the
    infant. The word "Seer" cracks Sorry's possession for a heartbeat. She cannot kill Crokus.
12. Kruppe reads of the **Crippled God**. Hairlock burns Great Ravens out of the sky with chaos.
    Quick Ben walks into Shadow to deal — and Shadowthrone recognises him by an older name.
13. Guild assassins and Tiste Andii ambush Kalam and Quick Ben; **Anomander Rake** arrives and ends
    it, killing the demon **Pearl**. Crokus returns the stolen jewels to Challice's balcony.

## Book Five — The Gadrobi Hills (Ch. 14–16)
14. Tool reveals the mission: free the **Jaghut Tyrant** to bait Rake away from the city. He was
    chosen because he is clanless — expendable to his own kind.
15. Hairlock ambushes Paran; Toc is torn into a warren and vanishes. Quick Ben lures the **Hounds**;
    Rake kills two of them. Paran is drawn into **Dragnipur** and sees the chained dead hauling the
    wagon. Shadowthrone recalls Cotillion; the possession lifts and the fisher girl wakes with no
    memory of two years.
16. Crokus rides with the wrecked girl, who takes the name **Apsalar**. Paran meets a five-year-old
    Rhivi child wearing Tattersail's soul. Paran and **Coll** recognise each other as exiles.

## Book Six — The City of Blue Fire (Ch. 17–19)
17. Rallick coats himself in magic-deadening powder. Rake admits to Baruk that his people are dying
    of purposelessness. **Serrat** moves on the Coinbearer.
18. **Fiddler** tells Whiskeyjack to stop rationing his own grief. Rallick kills his clan leader
    Ocelot in **K'rul's Belfry** and is left dying. Paran reaches the city with wounded Coll.
    Whiskeyjack names the real threat coming from the south: the **Pannion Seer**. Lorn plants the
    **Finnest** — the Tyrant's power in the shape of an acorn — in Lady Simtal's garden.
19. Crokus and Apsalar climb the belfry; the powder has drunk the temple's power and healed Rallick
    whole. Kruppe confirms to Baruk that he is the Eel. Lorn hunts a Sorry who no longer exists.

## Book Seven — The Fête (Ch. 20–24) — the Convergence
20. Everyone converges on **Lady Simtal's Fête**. **Raest** attacks Kruppe in dream and is blocked
    by Tool and K'rul. **Vorcan** takes the contract on the entire T'orrud Cabal.
21. Turban Orr forces a duel on Rallick; **Rake stands as second**; Orr dies. Simtal is destroyed
    socially and Coll's ruin is avenged. Challice mistakes Crokus for someone else.
22. Raest wakes, takes **Mammot**, and tears the terrace apart. Quick Ben opens **seven warrens at
    once** and collapses. **Hedge** puts a cusser exactly where it needs to go. An **Azath** rises
    out of the ground and takes the Tyrant. The city's gas mains are one spark from ending
    everything.
23. Cotillion sets a Hound on Paran. Lorn corners Crokus and is killed in the street by the Eel's
    people. Rake goes dragon against a Galayn demon and feeds it to Dragnipur.
24. Rake wins. Crokus stops Vorcan from finishing Baruk. Dujek's army is formally outlawed and
    becomes free of the Empress. **Moon's Spawn** withdraws south.

## Epilogue
- Paran feels Tattersail's presence in the reborn child.
- Kalam, Apsalar and Crokus sail for Itko Kan to take a girl home.
- Crokus throws **Oponn's Coin** into the sea. Whatever was steering stops steering.

## Adaptation notes
- **Natural act breaks:** Pale (1–4) · Arrival in Darujhistan (5–7) · The Mission (8–10) ·
  Assassins (11–13) · The Hills (14–16) · The City (17–19) · The Fête (20–24).
- **Natural convergences (= bottlenecks):** end of Ch. 4, Ch. 7, Ch. 10, Ch. 13, Ch. 16, Ch. 19,
  Ch. 22, Ch. 24.
- **Best set pieces for tense, short-page pacing:** the alley stabbing (3), Hood's Gate (4), the
  Bellurdan detonation (10), the Hound hunt (15), the belfry (18–19), the Fête (22–24).
- **Best set pieces for long, atmospheric pages:** Lorn and Tool crossing the plain (9, 14),
  Kruppe's dreams (5, 7, 11, 12), Rake and Baruk's conversations (6, 17).
