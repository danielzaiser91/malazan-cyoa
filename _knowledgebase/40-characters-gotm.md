# Characters — Gardens of the Moon

`#spoiler:gotm`. Voice notes are adaptation guidance, not canon quotes.

## The Bridgeburners

| Name | Role | Voice / handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Whiskeyjack** | Sergeant, demoted commander, Old Guard | Says a third of what he thinks. Grief carried as flat competence. Never raises his voice. |
| **Quick Ben** | Squad mage, holds twelve warrens | Cocky, improvises with dead men's souls, always three plans deep. Comic on the surface, ruthless under it. |
| **Kalam** | Corporal, ex-Claw assassin | Calm, enormous, dry. The only one Quick Ben tells the truth to. |
| **Fiddler** | Sapper | Pessimist with perfect aim; the emotional conscience of the squad. Hates warren travel. |
| **Hedge** | Sapper | Fiddler's partner in demolition and bickering. |
| **Trotts** | Barghast warrior | Grins. Rarely speaks. Terrifying. |
| **Mallet** | Squad healer (Denul) | Tired kindness; keeps a private ledger of who he could not save. |
| **Sorry** | Recruit — actually **Cotillion** riding a possessed fisher girl | Uncanny stillness, absolute violence, no small talk. After the possession lifts she is **Apsalar**, a frightened teenager with a killer's reflexes and no memories. Handle with care: her arc is the book's cruellest. |
| **Ganoes Paran** | Captain, noble-born, Tavore's brother | Wants to be a soldier and is being used as a piece by three separate powers. The reader's entry point. Ends the book knowing far more than is good for him. |

## The Empire

- **Empress Laseen** — offstage, present in every decision. Purging the Old Guard.
- **Adjunct Lorn** — the Empress's blade. Otataral sword, no sorcery touches her. Believes she has
  no self left outside her function. Her long walk with Tool is the best material in the book for
  quiet, dialogue-driven CYOA scenes.
- **High Mage Tayschrenn** — killed friendly mages at Pale. Deliberately? Ambiguous by design.
  **Never resolve this ambiguity in the game.**
- **Dujek Onearm** — High Fist. Pragmatic, beloved, about to be outlawed as a manoeuvre.
- **Tattersail** — 2nd Army cadre sorceress, survivor of Pale, reader of the Deck. Middle-aged,
  self-deprecating about her weight, brave in the way of people who expect to die. Her ending
  (soul reborn into a Rhivi child) is one of the book's structural surprises.
- **Hairlock** — a mage's soul sewn into a wooden puppet. Goes wrong. Then worse.
- **Toc the Younger** — Claw-born messenger, one eye, good humour, vanishes into a warren tear.
- **Bellurdan / Nightchill** — Thelomen giant and his mage partner; her death and his grief drive
  Chapter 10's catastrophe.

## Darujhistan

- **Kruppe** — fat, self-referential, speaks of himself in third person, dreams with Elder Gods,
  is secretly the Eel. **The game's narrator.** Comic voice that carries the book's warmest
  humanity. Getting Kruppe wrong ruins the adaptation; getting him right sells it.
- **Crokus Younghand** — teenage thief, in love with the idea of a noblewoman. Carries Oponn's
  Coin. The closest thing to an innocent.
- **Rallick Nom** — assassin, Guild, moral in a way that makes no sense to anyone else.
- **Murillio** — duellist, charm, vendetta partner to Rallick.
- **Coll** — drunk, ruined nobleman, better than everyone thinks.
- **Baruk** — High Alchemist, holds the city's secrets and the alliance with Rake.
- **Mammot** — scholar, Crokus's uncle, High Priest of D'rek — and the vessel the Tyrant takes.
- **Vorcan** — Mistress of the Assassins' Guild. Contract on the entire Cabal.
- **Circle Breaker**, **Meese**, **Irilta** — the Eel's small, human network.
- **Turban Orr / Lady Simtal / Challice D'Arle** — the Council plot and the Fête.

## The other powers

- **Anomander Rake** — Son of Darkness, Lord of Moon's Spawn, Soletaken black dragon, bearer of
  **Dragnipur**. Weary, courteous, catastrophically dangerous. Underwrite him: understatement is
  the whole effect.
- **Caladan Brood** — warlord, hammer that could break the earth, would rather not.
- **Crone** — Great Raven, Rake's messenger, delighted by everything terrible.
- **Onos T'oolan (Tool)** — clanless T'lan Imass. Lorn's escort and, slowly, her only friend.
- **Raest** — the Jaghut Tyrant in the barrow. Ancient, contemptuous, ends up gardening for an
  Azath.
- **Serrat** — Rake's Tiste Andii assassin, tasked with the Coinbearer.
- **Shadowthrone, Cotillion, the Hounds** — the manipulators.
- **Oponn** — the Twins. Their coin cuts both ways every single time.

## Point-of-view distribution (matters for the game's PC design)

Book 1 rotates roughly between: Paran, Tattersail, Whiskeyjack's squad, Crokus, Kruppe, Lorn/Tool,
Rallick/Murillio, and short god-level interludes. **No single character is present at more than
about a third of the plot.** Any adaptation with one fixed PC must either invent presence or
switch POV — see `../_reference/05-game-design-plan.md` § Player character.
