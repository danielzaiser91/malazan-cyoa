# Races, Peoples and Factions

`#spoiler:gotm` — everything here is safe for Book 1 content unless marked `[later]`.

## Elder races

| Race | What matters |
|---|---|
| **Imass** | Neanderthal-analogue humans. Most of them performed the **Ritual of Tellann** and became **T'lan Imass**: undead, dust-dry, effectively immortal, bound to a war against the Jaghut that ended long ago and never stopped. They can collapse into dust and travel as it. Emotionally hollowed but not empty — the tragedy is that they remember what they gave up. |
| **Jaghut** | Solitary, long-lived, immensely powerful, users of **Omtose Phellack** (ice). Mostly harmless recluses; a minority became **Tyrants** — and the Imass exterminated the entire species over it. The moral asymmetry is the point. |
| **K'Chain Che'Malle** | Reptilian, matriarchal, builders of sky-keeps and of the **K'ell Hunters**; their civilisation collapsed long before the books. |
| **Forkrul Assail** | Arbiters. They judge, and their judgement is annihilation delivered in a calm voice. `[later — barely present in Book 1]` |
| **Tiste Andii** | Children of Mother Dark, black-skinned, silver/white-haired, long-lived, culturally exhausted. Warren: **Kurald Galain**. Led in Book 1 by **Anomander Rake**. Their defining problem is not war but the loss of any reason to keep going. |
| **Tiste Edur** / **Tiste Liosan** | Shadow and Light siblings of the Andii. `[later]` |
| **Eleint (dragons)** | Pure sorcery given form; **Soletaken** are shapeshifters who take one alternate form, **D'ivers** split into many creatures. |

## Younger peoples

- **Barghast** — nomadic warrior clans, ancestor-worship, founder-spirits carried as totems.
- **Rhivi** — plains nomads of Genabackis, herders of bhederin, allied with Brood.
- **Trell** — large, tusked, mostly broken as a people; Mappo is the famous one `[later]`.
- **Moranth** — Genabackan, insectile armour, colour-coded castes (**Black**, Gold, Red, Green,
  Silver, Blue). They supply the Malazans with **munitions** — sharpers, burners, crackers,
  flamers and the huge **cussers**. Quorls (giant dragonflies) as air transport.
- **Malazans** — the empire's people, but the word mostly means the army.

## The Malazan Empire

Founded by **Emperor Kellanved** and his partner **Dancer**, both assassinated (allegedly) by
**Surly**, commander of the Claw, who took the throne as **Empress Laseen**. The joke the world has
not caught up with: Kellanved and Dancer did not die — they ascended as **Shadowthrone** and
**Cotillion (the Rope)**.

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **The Claw** | Imperial assassin-mage corps and secret police. Feared, resented by the army. |
| **The Old Guard** | Kellanved's veterans — Dujek, Whiskeyjack, Dassem Ultor. Laseen has been quietly purging them. |
| **Dujek Onearm's Host / 2nd Army** | The Genabackis campaign force. |
| **The Bridgeburners** | Legendary elite squad-of-squads inside the 2nd, once thousands, now ~40 after Pale. Saboteurs and sappers. Their reputation is a liability: the Empress wants the legend dead. |
| **The Adjunct** | The Empress's personal instrument, immune to sorcery via an **otataral** sword. In Book 1: **Adjunct Lorn**. |
| **High Mage Tayschrenn** | The empire's most powerful sorcerer. Whether he betrayed the Bridgeburners at Pale is the book's central open question. |

## Genabackis — the other side

- **The Free Cities** — a loose collection of independent city-states being eaten one by one. Pale
  has just fallen. **Darujhistan** is the largest and richest and is next.
- **Caladan Brood's alliance** — the warlord holding the north with the **Crimson Guard** (Prince
  K'azz's mercenaries under an eternal vow against the Empire), Rhivi, Barghast, and Rake's Andii.
- **Anomander Rake / Moon's Spawn** — a floating mountain fortress and the Son of Darkness who
  rules it. Wields **Dragnipur**, a sword whose victims are chained forever to a wagon dragging
  Darkness itself. Not an ally of Brood so much as a force pointed in the same direction.
- **The Pannion Domin** — a theocratic cannibal empire rising in the south. In Book 1 it is a
  rumour with a bad smell. It becomes Book 3.

## Darujhistan, city of blue fire

Lit by natural gas piped under the streets — literally sitting on something that can burn.

| Group | Who |
|---|---|
| **The Council** | Merchant-oligarchs, endlessly scheming; Turban Orr, Lady Simtal, Coll (ruined by them). |
| **The T'orrud Cabal** | The city's secret circle of mages; **Baruk** the High Alchemist is its face. |
| **The Assassins' Guild** | Run by **Vorcan**, the world's best killer. Rallick Nom is one of hers. |
| **The Phoenix Inn regulars** | Kruppe, Rallick Nom, Murillio, Crokus, Coll, Meese, Irilta, Sulty. The heart of the book. |
| **The Eel** | The city's master spy. Everyone hunts the Eel. The Eel is Kruppe. |

## Gods and Ascendants active in Book 1

**Shadowthrone (Ammanas)** and **Cotillion (the Rope)** with their **Hounds of Shadow** —
manipulating from the first page. **Oponn**, the Twin Jesters of Chance (Lady who Pulls, Lord who
Pushes) — the coin, and the plot. **Hood**, Lord of Death, and his gate. **K'rul**, Elder God whose
blood is the warrens, waking because old rituals are being disturbed. **Fener** the Boar of Summer,
**Burn** the Sleeping Goddess, **Treach**, **the Queen of Dreams**, **Caladan Brood** and
**Anomander Rake** as Ascendants who mostly refuse the game.
