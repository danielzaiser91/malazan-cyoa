# Style and Voice Guide

`#spoiler:none`. Binding for all game text, DE and EN.

## The rule above all rules

**Write original prose.** Not one sentence is lifted from the novels. We are retelling events, and
events are not copyrightable — sentences are. If a passage feels like it is reaching for a
remembered line, rewrite it.

## Register

- **Third person, past tense** for scene prose. Second person ("you") only inside choice labels
  and system text.
- Short declaratives under pressure. Long, clause-heavy sentences when a character is thinking,
  grieving, or when the world is being described from above.
- **Understate the enormous.** A god arriving gets fewer adjectives than a soldier's boots. This is
  the single most Malazan stylistic move there is.
- No neat morals at the end of a scene. Cut the last sentence if it explains the scene.
- Humour lands **immediately after** horror, from characters who have run out of any other way to
  cope. Never authorial winking.
- Proper nouns without apology: a Barghast, a bhederin, a Soletaken — the codex catches the player,
  the prose does not stop.

## Voices to hit

| Character | Signature |
|---|---|
| Kruppe | Third person about himself, endless self-flattery, hides real intelligence under waffle. "Kruppe observes…" |
| Whiskeyjack | Fewest words in any room. Silence as answer. |
| Quick Ben | Fast, jokey, always one move he hasn't mentioned. |
| Fiddler | Complains, delivers, complains about having delivered. |
| Anomander Rake | Courteous, tired, understated. Threat by omission. |
| Tattersail | Self-deprecating, warm, exhausted, brave without noticing. |
| Lorn | Controlled to the point of brittleness. Cracks show only to Tool. |
| Tool | Flat, factual, ancient. The occasional sentence that breaks your heart because it isn't trying to. |

## German localisation

- DE is a **first-class translation**, never machine-slop. Same beats, same length band, natural
  German rhythm — not English word order in German words.
- **Established German terms** from the Blanvalet translations where they exist and are widely
  recognised; otherwise keep the original term and gloss it in the codex once.
  Decide once, record in `_reference/terminology-de.md`, never vary afterwards.
- Proper names (Whiskeyjack, Moon's Spawn / Mondbrut, Bridgeburners / Brückenverbrenner) are a
  **terminology decision, not a per-page decision**. Lock the glossary before writing chapter text.
- Duzen/Siezen: system text duzt den Spieler. In-world dialogue follows the character's rank and
  relationship.

## Content warnings

The source material contains massacre, torture, slavery, sexual violence (mostly off-page),
cannibalism and child endangerment. The game keeps the weight and stays off-page where the novels
are on-page. A content-warning screen is required before the first chapter and must be reachable
from settings.
